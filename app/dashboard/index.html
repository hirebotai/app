Frequently asked questions
Where does my cheat sheet appear?
Press Alt+N anywhere to toggle the HUD overlay. Your notes render in the floating HUD window and auto-save as you type.
Which API key do I need for screen capture?
OpenRouter or Gemini — both support vision models. Groq is text-only and won't power screen capture, so pair it with a vision provider.
Hotkeys stopped working, what now?
Make sure Master Power is on, and no other app is bound to the same combination. Reassign the shortcut from the Hotkeys page.
How do I keep my trial active?
Use "Extend Trial" on the API Keys page to add time, or activate a license key to unlock all features permanently.
Where is my session history stored?
Every mock interview and practice run is saved locally under your profile. Open the History page to replay transcripts, review scorecards, or copy a session's diagnostics.
How do interview questions use my resume?
Upload a resume and set it as active on the Resume page, then flip on "Use my resume" before starting a session. The AI will tailor questions to your listed skills and experience.
What happens when my trial expires?
The engine pauses and the dashboard shows a "trial expired — license required" state. Your notes, history, and settings stay intact, so nothing is lost once you extend the trial or activate a key.
How do I switch the AI provider?
Open the API Keys page and enable your preferred provider — OpenRouter, Gemini, or Groq. Screen capture needs a vision-capable provider (OpenRouter or Gemini), while text chat can run on any.
Can I replay or export a practice session?
Yes. Open the session from the History page to scroll the full transcript, then use "Copy Diagnostics" on the Support page to export the details for review or support.
How many questions can I set per interview?
Pick 3 to 30 questions when configuring a session. Higher counts give a broader range of topics but take longer — 5 to 10 is a good balance for most practice runs.
Does the HUD cheat sheet work everywhere?
The HUD overlay works on any screen where you've enabled it. Toggle it per-tab from the extension popup, and adjust the shortcut from the Hotkeys page if it conflicts with another app.
Still stuck?
The fastest way to a fix is an email with your diagnostics attached.

How to Use This Tool — Read Before Your First Interview / Exam

🎯 What This Tool Can Do
• Restricted Apps / Exams: Open the HUD inside any restricted app or exam window. It stays 100% invisible to screen recorders, Zoom, Teams, and proctoring capture APIs. Use it to solve questions silently.

• Live Interviews: Use Interview Mode in live video interviews. The AI listens to the interviewer's audio and gives you live, spoken answers. Only you see the HUD; the interviewer sees nothing.

• Anywhere, Anytime: Press Alt+S to capture the current screen and solve it instantly. Works from any app where the HUD is open — browsers, IDEs, exam portals, video calls.

• Invisible & Undetectable: Ghost Mode makes the HUD click-through. The window is hidden from Alt+Tab and spoofed as a benign Windows component. Proctoring software cannot see or detect it.
🛡️ Critical Rules — Follow These Exactly
• Never toggle Ghost Mode OFF in restricted exams. If the HUD becomes clickable, it can be detected. Keep Ghost Mode ON at all times during exams.

• Don't take screenshots continuously. Wait at least 30 seconds between Alt+S captures. Rapid screenshots look like automated behavior and may be flagged.

• Upload your resume BEFORE entering Interview Mode. The AI uses your resume to generate personalized answers. Without it, answers will be generic and less convincing.

• Check your license and trial status BEFORE the exam/interview. Make sure the dashboard shows your license is active. An expired trial mid-session will break the engine.

• Keep the HUD on the same monitor as your interview/exam window. Moving it to another screen or minimizing it can cause focus issues.
📸 How Alt+S Works (Capture / Text)
No text selected: Alt+S takes a screenshot of the active window and sends it to the AI. The answer appears in the HUD.

Text highlighted/selected: Alt+S sends ONLY the highlighted text to the AI. No screenshot is taken. This is useful when you want to ask about specific content without capturing the whole screen.

In Chat Mode (Alt+Q): Alt+S behaves differently depending on what you have selected:
• If nothing is selected → screenshot is attached to the current chat message.
• If text is highlighted → the highlighted text is inserted into the chat input instead of a screenshot.
• You can then edit the text, add more context, and press Enter to submit. Esc cancels the chat mode.

Tip: Use text selection when you only need to solve a specific paragraph or code snippet. Use screenshot when you need the AI to see the full layout, diagram, or image.
💬 How Chat Mode Works (Alt+Q)
Chat Mode lets you type questions to the AI without showing the HUD. This is useful when you need to ask something discreetly without the HUD being visible on screen.

How to use:
1. Press Alt+Q to enter Chat Mode.
2. Start typing your question. Your keystrokes are suppressed from the active window, so the app behind the HUD won't receive them.
3. Press Alt+S while typing to attach a screenshot of the current window to your question.
4. If you have text highlighted before pressing Alt+S, that text is inserted into your chat message instead of a screenshot.
5. Press Enter to submit your question to the AI.
6. Press Esc to cancel and exit Chat Mode.

Example: You're in an exam browser and want to ask about a specific question. Highlight the question text, press Alt+Q, then press Alt+S — the highlighted text is sent to AI. Or press Alt+Q, type your question, press Alt+S to attach the full screen, then Enter to submit.
🎭 Features & How They Work
Ghost Mode (Alt+T, default ON): HUD is click-through. Mouse passes through to the interview window. The HUD is visible only to you, not to screen captures.

Dormant Mode (Alt+Z): HUD hides completely. Audio stops. All hotkeys are suppressed EXCEPT Alt+Z. Use this to pause everything instantly.

Stealth Chat (Alt+Q): Type queries without showing the HUD. Keystrokes are suppressed from the active window. Alt+S attaches screenshot or selected text. Enter submits, Esc cancels.

Interview Mode (Alt+I): AI listens to interviewer audio via microphone and gives you live answers. Shows interviewer questions + AI suggestions only. Your voice is hidden.

Capture & Solve (Alt+S): Takes a screenshot of the active window, sends it to AI, and shows the answer in the HUD.

HUD Controls (if enabled in Settings): Opacity slider, screenshot button, mic toggle, and text size dropdown appear directly on the HUD header.
